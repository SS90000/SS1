How this register is built

Regulator-publishedtaken from the regulator's own list Company-reportedfrom the sponsor's announcement Unconfirmedthird-party calendar, awaiting confirmation

United States: FDA's Novel Drug Approvals list (new molecular entities and new therapeutic biologics). Pending decisions use the PDUFA target date, confirmed against company announcements where available.

Singapore: HSA's Listing of approvals, new drug applications (NDA-1 and NDA-2). Additional strengths of a product already registered (NDA-3) are folded into the product. HSA does not publish indications in this list.

Australia: coming soon.

Submission dates are rarely published by regulators. They are added when a regulator or sponsor discloses them. This is a pilot; check the linked source before relying on any date.
